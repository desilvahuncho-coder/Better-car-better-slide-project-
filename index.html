Check my car

Carzz helps drivers understand what their car is trying to tell them. Choose a sound, a warning light, a door problem or a leak, and see the likely causes, how urgent it is and a rough repair cost. Then find garages near you.

What do you want to check?

SoundsGrinding, squealing, knocking and more Lights, doors & leaksWarning lights, locks, windows, fluid spots

How it works

  1. Pick what you hear, see or notice.
  2. Get likely causes, urgency and a rough cost in KSh.
  3. Contact a garage near you on WhatsApp or Maps.
